# 2-Minute Demo Recording Script

Target duration: **1 minute 45 seconds**. A plain screen recording is enough.

## 0:00–0:12 — The problem

Show the landing view and say:

> “RUET students study from scattered PDFs, notes, question banks, and videos. The hard part is not finding content—it is understanding what matters and knowing what to do next.”

## 0:12–0:24 — The solution

Point to the material panel and the three tabs:

> “StudySprint turns one course material into a complete study loop: understand, plan, practice, and repair weak topics.”

Show the built-in Operating Systems sample. Mention that students can upload a PDF/TXT/Markdown file or paste notes.

## 0:24–0:52 — Grounded course assistant

Open **Ask your notes** and click the prompt “Context switch সহজভাবে বুঝিয়ে দাও.”

While the result appears, say:

> “Gemini 3.6 Flash reads the selected material and answers from that material—not from an unrelated generic response.”

Point to:

- the simple explanation;
- exam-ready key points;
- confidence and material connection;
- three YouTube resources: explanation, Bangla tutorial, and practice.

Open one YouTube resource briefly to show that it leads to a real targeted search.

## 0:52–1:17 — Personalized study plan

Open **Study plan**, select **7 days**, and generate.

Say:

> “StudySprint identifies high-priority topics and creates a realistic daily plan with concept learning, numerical practice, and revision. Every priority topic also receives a focused video resource.”

Scroll through priority cards, daily schedule, and the quick win.

## 1:17–1:38 — Active-recall quiz

Open **Practice quiz** and start the quiz. Select one wrong option on purpose.

Say:

> “The quiz tests understanding, gives an immediate explanation, tracks the score, and recommends a targeted video when the student gets a topic wrong.”

## 1:38–1:48 — Gemini and impact

Return to the top and say:

> “Gemini is the core of StudySprint: it understands documents, explains, prioritizes, plans, assesses, and connects students to the next useful resource. StudySprint gives every RUET student a clearer next study move.”

## Recording checklist

- Use a 1440×900 or 1920×1080 browser window.
- Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs.
- Use the sample material so the flow is predictable.
- Make sure `.env.local` contains a valid, newly rotated Gemini key.
- Test all three actions before recording.
- Keep the final video below 2 minutes.
- Upload it somewhere judges can access without requesting permission.
